# For all my niggas

| **Afan Oromo**                                                                                                                                       | **English**                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ogbarruun dameelee gurguddoo lama qaba: ogbarruu barreeffamaa fi afoola jedhamuun beekamu.**                                                       | **Literature has two major branches: written literature and oral literature.**                                                                            |
| **Ogbarruun barreeffamaa kanneen barreeffamaan mul'atan yommuu ta'an kanneen akka asoosamaa, walaloofi diraamaa of jalatti hammata.**                | **Written literature includes works presented in written form such as fiction, poetry, and drama.**                                                       |
| **Afoolli immoo kanneen afaaniin dhalootarraa dhalootatti darbanidha (Fkn. oduu durii, mammaaksa, hibboo, tapha ijoollee, sirba, geerarsaafi kkf).** | **Oral literature is passed from generation to generation by word of mouth (e.g., folktales, proverbs, riddles, children's games, songs, chants, etc.).** |
| **Gosoota ogbarruu barreeffamaa keessaa tokko asoosamadha.**                                                                                         | **One type of written literature is fiction.**                                                                                                            |
| **Asoosamni immoo dameelee gabaabaafi dheerratti bakka lamatti caba.**                                                                               | **Fiction is divided into short and long forms.**                                                                                                         |
| **Kanaafuu, asoosamni dheeraan gosoota asoosamaa keessaa tokko ta'ee kan seeneffamaan mul'atudha.**                                                  | **Therefore, a novel is a type of fiction presented through narration.**                                                                                  |

---

### **Characteristics of a Novel**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Asoosama dheeraan hojii kalaqa dhala namaa ta'ee kan barreessaan dhugaa hawaasa keessa jiruufi muuxannoo ofiisaa bu'uura godhachuun hidhata sababaafi bu'aan seenaa qindeessudha.**|**A novel is a creative human work in which the author organizes a story based on real social life and personal experience using cause-and-effect relationships.**|
|**Asoosamni dheeraan asoosama gabaabaa caalaa seenaa dheeraa qabaachuu nidanda'a.**|**A novel can have a longer storyline than short fiction.**|
|**Waa'ee dhimma tokkos dhalootaa hanga du'aatti seenessuuf ga'umsa qaba.**|**It can narrate a matter from birth to death.**|
|**Yoomessa adda addaa, namfakkii hedduu, waldiddaa falli isaa yeroo dheeraa fudhatu gargaaramuun jaargochasaa babal'ifachuuf bakkaafi yeroo bal'aa qaba.**|**It has a broad scope to expand its plot using varied settings, many characters, and long-resolved conflicts.**|
|**Seeneffama kalaqaa jiruufi jireenya dhala namaa bu'uureffachuun dhala namaan dhala namaaf qophaa'udha.**|**It is created by humans for humans, based on imaginative narration and human life.**|

---

### **Elements of a Novel**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Caacculee asoosamaa jechuun qaamota asoosama tokko hiika qabeessa taasisuuf barbaachisoo ta'an ykn qaamota asoosamni tokko irraa ijaaramu jechuudha.**|**Elements of fiction are the components necessary to make a story meaningful or the parts from which it is constructed.**|
|**Caacculee asoosamaa: jaargocha, namfakkii, waldiddaa, yoomessa, ijoo ergaafi ija.**|**Elements include: plot, character, conflict, setting, theme, and point of view.**|

---

### **Key Literary Elements**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Jaargocha:** Akka lafee dugdaa asoosamaatti ilaalama; seenaa sababaafi bu'aan waliin qindeessa.|**Plot:** The backbone of a story; organizes events with cause-and-effect relationships.|
|**Namfakkii:** Namoota ykn wantoota seeneffama keessatti qooda fudhatan.|**Character:** People or entities that participate in the story.|
|**Yoomessa:** Haala fi yeroo seenaa itti raawwatamu.|**Setting:** The time and place where the story occurs.|
|**Waldiddaa:** Walitti bu'iinsa namfakkiilee.|**Conflict:** The clash or struggle between characters.|
|**Ergaa:** Dhaamsa handhuuraa barreessaan dabarsu.|**Theme:** The central message conveyed by the author.|
|**Ija:** Kallattii seenaan irraa himamu.|**Point of View:** The perspective from which the story is told.|

### **Punctuation Overview**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Tuqaaleen mallattoowwan barreeffama keessatti iddoo olaanaa qabu.**|**Punctuation marks play an important role in writing.**|
|**Sirnaan itti fayyadamuu baannaan ergaan ni jallachuu danda'a.**|**If not used properly, the message can become unclear.**|

---

### **Comma (,)**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Waantota tarreeffaman adda baasa.**|**Separates items in a list.**|
|**Guyyaa fi bara adda baasa.**|**Separates date and year.**|
|**Nama kallattiin waamamu adda baasa.**|**Separates the addressed person.**|
|**Ciroowwan adda baasa.**|**Separates clauses.**|
|**Walqabsiistota booda gala.**|**Used after conjunctions/connectors.**|
|**Ibsitoota adda baasa.**|**Separates appositives.**|
|**Seensistoota booda gala.**|**Used after introductory elements.**|

---

### **Semicolon (;)**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Ciroowwan ofdanda'oo lama gidduu gala.**|**Links two independent clauses.**|
|**Qabsiistota dura gala.**|**Used before conjunctive adverbs (e.g., however).**|
|**Himoota qoodduu qaban waliin tajaajila.**|**Used with sentences containing commas.**|
|**Yaadolee walitti dhiyaatan adda baasa.**|**Separates closely related ideas.**|
|**Yaada dabalataa ibsa.**|**Adds further explanation.**|

---

### **Quotation Marks**

|**Afan Oromo**|**English**|
|---|---|
|**Mallattoo waraabbii dacha (" "): yaada kallattiin ibsu.**|**Double quotation marks: indicate direct speech or quotation.**|
|**Mallattoo waraabbii qeentee: waraabbii keessaa waraabbii agarsiisa.**|**Single quotation marks: indicate a quotation within a quotation.**|
